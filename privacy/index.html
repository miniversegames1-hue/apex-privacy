Apex Privacy Policy

Updated: 4 October 2026

About this policy

Apex is an offline single-player game developed by Semih Gülücü (Miniverse Games). This policy describes the app, this privacy website and voluntary contact with us.

Data in the app

Game progress, rewards, inventory, your chosen snake name, appearance and sound/haptic preferences are stored on your device. No developer account is required. All seven regions are free through normal guardian progression. The optional one-time Ad-free Pack purchase and restoration use Apple StoreKit and your Apple Account; verified previous Full Game purchases also grant the pack. Apple processes payments and transactions under its privacy policy. We do not receive card details. Restoration recovers cosmetic ownership, not your game save. Optional ad availability depends on the app version and configuration; when ads are disabled, the app makes no ad requests. There is no remote analytics service or developer gameplay upload. Optional playtest recording is off by default. If enabled, it stores dates, daily event counts, language, app version and a test cohort locally, without an account or unique player identifier. Only your explicit export action creates a report for you to share; if you send it to us, we receive the information it contains. The pack disables all ad offers and ad requests and grants three permanent looks. Unlimited gameplay remains free without purchase.

Apple Privacy Policy

Optional ads and privacy choices

Optional video ads let you try a selected cosmetic look for one hunt, up to three completed rewards per day. Declining or closing an ad does not block the free campaign. When you choose to watch, Google Mobile Ads and UMP process consent and advertising requests. Google may process IP address, device identifiers, diagnostics, performance, ad views and interactions. We request non-personalized ads; this does not mean no data is processed. Where required, you can change your choice in advertising privacy options. See Google Privacy Policy.

Google and its advertising partners may infer approximate location from your IP address and process data to deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud. Providers and purposes are listed in the consent form. Where required, consent and privacy choices govern permitted processing. Apex does not request precise GPS location. Providers handle retention and deletion under their own privacy policies. You can manage available advertising privacy choices in the game settings.

Storage and deletion

A previous valid local save is kept for recovery if the latest save is damaged. We do not provide developer-managed cloud saves or synchronization. Operating-system or user-enabled device/iCloud backups may include app data and may restore it later. Uninstalling the app removes its local data from that installation; it does not necessarily remove existing backups. Manage or delete backups through your device or backup provider.

This website

This policy website is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you visit it, GitHub may process technical request information, such as your IP address, under its own privacy statement. Our page adds no analytics, tracking cookies or advertising. Language is selected through the URL query; the page does not need to save a language preference.

Contact

If you choose to email miniverse.games1@gmail.com, we receive your email address and whatever information you include, and may use it to respond to your request. Your email provider and ours may process that correspondence. Please avoid sending sensitive information or unnecessary personal data.

We retain contact correspondence only as needed to respond to and resolve your request. You can request deletion of that correspondence by emailing miniverse.games1@gmail.com.

Updates

We may update this policy when the app or its data handling changes. The updated date appears above. For privacy questions, contact Semih Gülücü (Miniverse Games) at miniverse.games1@gmail.com.
